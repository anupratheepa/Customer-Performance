**Customer Performance Dashboard**

This Power BI dashboard provides actionable insights into customer behavior, product performance, and purchasing trends, enabling data-driven decision-making. The analysis leverages customer demographics, geography, and sales data to uncover patterns that drive revenue and profitability.

**🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

1. Total Revenue: $307M
2. Total Sales: $29M
3. Profit: $123M
4. Revenue per Customer: $1.59K
5. Revenue (With Children): $217M
6. Revenue (Without Children): $90M

   **📈 Key Performance Metrics (KPMs)**
   
1. Revenue by Age Category: 41–50 age group contributes 74% of revenue, surpassing the average revenue line of $61.42M.
2. Customer Profile Segmentation: Loyal Customers, Periodic Customers, Very Low Purchase (VLP) Customers
3. Revenue Contribution by Country
4. Sales Trend Over Time
5. Top 5 Product Categories by Revenue


The Customer Performance Dashboard highlights that revenue generation is strongly influenced by customer demographics and product preferences. The majority of revenue is driven by customers aged 41–50, accounting for nearly three-quarters of total revenue, emphasizing this group as the key market segment. Customers with children represent a significant share of spending compared to those without, reinforcing the importance of family-oriented marketing strategies. Geographic analysis reveals uneven revenue contribution across countries, while sales trends showcase periods of growth opportunities. Product-level insights, particularly the Top 5 categories, identify the offerings most critical to profitability. Finally, customer segmentation into loyal, periodic, and very low purchase groups provides a roadmap for targeted engagement, enabling businesses to strengthen retention, boost profitability, and optimize resource allocation.
